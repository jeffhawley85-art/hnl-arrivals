Settings

Your key stays on this phone only. It is never put in the page link.
  1. Go to rapidapi.com, sign up and search AeroDataBox.
  2. Open Pricing and subscribe to Basic ($0).
  3. On the Playground tab, copy the X-RapidAPI-Key and paste it above.
The app always refreshes when you open it, if the data is more than 4 minutes old. Each refresh uses 2 API units.
The free Basic plan gives 600 units a month, about 300 refreshes. A tail that isn't found in the HNL list costs an extra 2 to 4 units to look up. That happens at most every 30 minutes, or every 5 minutes when you tap ↻.
If a plane shows no HNL arrival but FlightAware has one, tap Copy debug info and paste it to Claude. Your API key is not included.
